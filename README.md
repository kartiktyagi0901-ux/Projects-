# Finance Portfolio: Kartik Tyagi

Applied finance projects from my MSc International Finance at HWR Berlin, covering empirical asset pricing, valuation, macro and sector analysis, and personal-finance modelling.

| Project | What it shows | Methods | Links |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[Flood risk & insurer stock returns](https://github.com/kartiktyagi0901-ux/climate-risk-event-study)** | Whether Europe's three costliest floods (2000–2020) produced abnormal returns for Allianz, Munich Re and Swiss Re | Event study, CAPM market model, CAR, t-tests | Separate repo |
| **[SpaceX (SPCX) IPO valuation](spacex-ipo-valuation/)** | Whether the $1.75T IPO price is justified: bull vs. bear, scenarios, and the growth the price implies | Relative valuation, scenario & reverse valuation, sensitivity | Code + charts |
| **[Germany: structural winners, strugglers & finance](germany-structural-analysis/)** | 8 sectors × 6 structural drivers; whether German finance can fund the transition | Structural analysis, risk transmission, peer benchmarking | [Live page](https://kartiktyagi0901-ux.github.io/portfolio/germany-structural-analysis/) |
| **[Personal finance dashboard](personal-finance-dashboard/)** | Cash flow → savings rate → compounding projection | Annuity future value, real vs. nominal returns | [Live demo](https://kartiktyagi0901-ux.github.io/portfolio/personal-finance-dashboard/) |

**Tools:** Python (pandas, NumPy, statsmodels, matplotlib), Jupyter, Excel, HTML/JS for interactive analysis.
